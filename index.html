About

I build machine learning systems for real-world signals, turning AI research ideas into applications. Now I am moving that work toward ecology and biodiversity.

The core of my work was designing experiments at the intersection of different disciplines: machine learning, signal processing, computer vision and hardware engineering. I built machine learning systems end to end, for audio and video recognition, taking them out of simulation onto physical hardware that had to process signals in real time.

My professional journey brought me all around Europe in the past years of my life. I hold a PhD from the Université libre de Bruxelles, working in the LIQ group of Prof. Serge Massar. I was also affiliated with the OPERA group and fundeed by the Post-Digital European network. I was a visiting researcher at IBM Zurich and University of Gent. I did all of my studies at Polytechnic University of Turin, in Electronic Engineering.

Then, I spent most of 2025 in Latin America, volunteering with conservation projects in remote tropical forests of Honduras, Nicaragua, Peru and Bolivia: night surveys with pitfall and camera traps, nesting data for river turtles, anti-poaching patrols, science-education activities for children in local communities.

Now, I want to apply my technical skills to projects where AI addresses challenges with tangible benefit to society, towards problems with a clear scientific or societal purpose, beyond pure methodological research.

Italian and Belgian citizen. I work in Italian, English, Spanish and French.
